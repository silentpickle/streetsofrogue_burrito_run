## Decisions and deferred work

Inventory remains owner/container metadata, and tile geometry is selected-point detail. Renderer/viewport evidence is not final visible pixels; geometric LOS is not native awareness, hearing or reachability. Low progress does not prove a stuck actor; nearby doors do not prove a route.

Detailed window-damage/blocker instrumentation is deferred by user choice. Native wall/window LOS remains current evidence. Future bars policy blocks movement but permits sight, with the native result retained separately: **planned, not implemented/verified**. Dog food/hydrant desire/response, automatic route recovery, broader smell/hearing, additional species, live split-screen and network authority remain separate follow-ons. Cross-level pet persistence is “probably no.” A modest second machine is required before a support claim for that hardware class.

This strengthens WA-13 stationary evidence only; foreground, pressure/recovery, busy comparisons, allocations and modest hardware remain open.

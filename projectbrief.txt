PROJECT: Streets of Rogue 1.5 / More Bullshit Edition

GOAL

I want to explore whether Streets of Rogue 1 can be expanded into something that feels like “Streets of Rogue 1.5.”

I am NOT trying to recreate Streets of Rogue 2.

I am NOT trying to add:
- cars
- giant open-world systems
- farming
- weather simulation
- sprawling persistent-world mechanics
- complicated city simulation
- anything that explodes scope into a multi-year sequel

The core idea is:

Take Streets of Rogue 1 as it already exists, and dramatically increase the number of nouns, verbs, situations, delayed consequences, environmental interactions, and emergent stories.

The target feeling is:

“I have no idea what absurd thing is about to happen, but it emerged naturally from a bunch of simple systems colliding.”

Think:
- Streets of Rogue 1
- Mosa Lina
- immersive-sim problem solving
- systemic comedy
- occasional unfair/impossible situations are acceptable
- emergent stories are more important than perfect balance

I am explicitly OK with:
- some situations being unwinnable
- chaos
- balance weirdness
- accidental interactions
- NPCs doing stupid things
- systems colliding in unexpected ways

I do NOT want everything sanded down into polished, predictable content.

A useful design mantra:

“Add systems that create stories, not just content.”

--------------------------------------------------
CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------

1. EXPAND EXISTING SYSTEMS BEFORE INVENTING NEW ONES

If the game already understands:
- NPCs
- factions
- hostility
- ownership
- doors
- alarms
- hacking
- status effects
- bribery
- items
- explosions
- liquids
- electricity
- crime
- shops
- quests
- followers
- pathfinding
- witnesses
- cops
- gang relationships
- health
- poisoning
- traits
- charisma

then prefer adding new content that plugs into those systems.

Example:

Do not build a giant new simulation layer for a refrigerator.

Instead, make refrigerators interact with:
- hacking
- electricity
- ownership
- food
- poisoning
- explosions
- noise
- NPC reactions
- theft
- status effects

The goal is combinatorial depth.

2. ENVIRONMENT IS MORE IMPORTANT THAN MORE CHARACTER CLASSES

There are already lots of custom characters and workshop characters.

I am much more interested in:
- new buildings
- new interactive props
- new services
- new environmental hazards
- new delayed events
- new NPC appointments
- new reasons for existing NPCs to collide

3. DELAYED CONSEQUENCES ARE EXTREMELY VALUABLE

SOR already does immediate chaos well.

I want more:
“Wait, why is THAT GUY coming toward me?”

Examples:
- someone you called arrives later
- a delivery appears later
- a date eventually shows up
- a cop responds to a tip later
- an NPC you angered sends someone after you
- a courier gets intercepted
- a hired helper arrives after the situation has already changed

This is fertile territory for emergent comedy.

4. FAILURE SHOULD OFTEN BE BENIGN

If a weird combination happens:
- unsupported interaction -> nothing happens
- broken pathfinding -> log it
- strange outcome -> maybe funny
- impossible level -> acceptable
- balance disaster -> often acceptable

Do not over-engineer toward perfect solvability.

5. THE WORLD SHOULD FEEL MORE ALIVE, NOT MORE COMPLICATED TO CONTROL

I do NOT want:
- heavy crafting
- giant menus
- long progression trees
- complicated resource management
- MMO-style task systems

I DO want:
- more things happening
- more reasons to react
- more environmental surprise
- more strange NPC behavior
- more cascading consequences

--------------------------------------------------
SERVICES / PHONE APPS
--------------------------------------------------

This may be one of the most promising categories.

The phone could become a way to summon delayed chaos.

FOOD DELIVERY
Player orders food.

Later:
- courier spawns
- attempts to reach player
- can get lost
- can get robbed
- can die
- can be intercepted
- can trigger gang/cop reactions
- can show up during a fight
- food can be stolen
- wrong order possible
- courier may refuse dangerous location

This should physically occur in the game world.

AMAZON-LIKE DELIVERY SERVICE
Order items.

Later:
- delivery NPC appears
- attempts to deliver
- item may be intercepted
- cops may inspect suspicious package
- thief may steal it
- delivery may be dropped somewhere unsafe
- player may no longer be at location
- package can arrive during chaos

Possible items:
- medicine
- lockpicks
- bat
- electronics
- weird appliances
- food
- armor
- suspicious goods
- live animal
- oversized useless object

TASKRABBIT / HANDYMAN
Hire someone to:
- repair
- unlock
- install
- move object
- disable something
- sabotage something
- carry something

They physically arrive.

POLICE TIP LINE
Player anonymously reports:
- NPC
- building
- illegal activity

Potential for:
- framing enemies

BLACK MARKET DELIVERY
Order shady goods.

Possible outcomes:
- seller arrives
- police follow them
- rival gang follows them
- item is fake
- seller robs player

COURIER SERVICE
Send item to another NPC.

Could:
- lose it
- steal it
- die
- get intercepted
- deliver wrong item
- trigger quest consequences

--------------------------------------------------
DATING / HOOKUP APP
--------------------------------------------------

This is one of the strongest ideas.

The mechanic should use charisma.

Basic concept:

If player has sufficient charisma, they can swipe/use a dating app.

A matched NPC may agree to meet at:
- bar
- motel
- apartment
- restaurant
- random safe-ish location

The important thing:
Success is NOT binary.

The app exists to inject unpredictable NPCs into the world later.

Possible outcomes:

FLAKE
- says “omw”
- never arrives

LATE
- arrives much later than expected

NORMAL
- arrives
- hangs out
- may temporarily follow player

HELPFUL
- brings healing
- brings weapon
- brings item
- gives useful information

NEEDY
- immediately gives player a quest

THIEF
- tries to steal something

ROBBER
- arrives intending to rob player

COP
- secretly police
- may react to player crimes

CRIMINAL
- arrives while being chased

GANG MEMBER
- brings faction consequences

MARRIED
- jealous partner appears later

PARTY PERSON
- brings friends

CATFISH
- very different NPC type than expected

PSYCHO
- starts a fight

STALKER
- follows player after meeting

SCAMMER
- asks for money/item

RIVAL DATE
- becomes hostile if player invited someone else

MULTIPLE DATES
This should be allowed.

Example:
Player invites two people to same location.

Person A arrives first.

Person B arrives later.

They may:
- fight
- become friends
- ignore each other
- both turn on player
- attract gang/cops
- start separate quests

One can be a gang member.
One can belong to opposing gang.

That is exactly the kind of emergent mess I want.

Charisma should affect:
- chance of match
- quality of match
- likelihood of useful outcome
- maybe how many simultaneous matches are possible

But even high charisma should not guarantee safety.

--------------------------------------------------
MOSA LINA PRINCIPLE
--------------------------------------------------

I am interested in borrowing part of Mosa Lina’s philosophy:

Do not guarantee that every generated situation is cleanly solvable.

SOR already gives the player tools.

If a bizarre generated level creates:
- bad layout
- wrong tools
- hostile factions
- strange object combinations
- no obvious route

that may be OK.

I do NOT want the game to constantly rescue the player from weird generation.

Sometimes:
- you die
- you leave
- you improvise
- you discover an absurd exploit
- the level is effectively doomed

That can be part of the charm.

--------------------------------------------------
EMERGENT-STORY EXAMPLES
--------------------------------------------------

The kind of story I want:

“I ordered tacos. The delivery driver got mugged by a gang. I called the cops. My Tinder date showed up at the same time. She was secretly in the rival gang. The cops arrested her. I stole my tacos back and discovered the delivery driver had picked up my Amazon package too.”

Another:

“I broke an aquarium in the pet store. The water hit a damaged vending machine. A cop got shocked. Someone slipped on a shrimp. A dog ate the evidence.”

Another:

“I invited two dates to a motel. One was a thief and the other was a cop. The thief stole from the cop. I hid in a washing machine.”

That is the target experience.

TECHNICAL PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------

Before writing code:

1. Inspect the existing Streets of Rogue mod project.
2. Determine what framework this project currently uses.
3. Identify whether it is using:
   - BepInEx
   - Harmony
   - RogueLibs
   - custom patching
   - other mod framework
4. Map the existing extension points.
5. Identify what can be implemented cleanly without invasive engine rewrites.
6. Separate:
   - easy
   - medium
   - hard
   - probably-not-worth-it

I want architecture-first reconnaissance before implementation.

- NPCs
- buildings/chunks

- status effects
- interactions
- quests
- shops
- dialogue
- UI
- timed events
implemented?

C. Can we dynamically spawn NPCs after level start?

D. Can we schedule delayed events?

E. Can we create new phone/menu interactions?

F. Can we create new environmental objects?

G. Can new objects participate in:
- hacking
- damage

--------------------------------------------------
SCOPE RULE
--------------------------------------------------

Any proposed feature should be judged by:

“How many existing systems does this touch?”

High value:
One simple thing that interacts with many systems.

Low value:
One complicated thing that requires its own isolated subsystem.

Example:

GOOD:
Aquarium
Because it touches:
- damage
- water
- electricity
- pets
- NPC reactions
- ownership
- shopkeepers

BAD:
Full realistic fish simulation
Because it adds complexity without creating many new stories.

GOOD:
Food delivery
Because it touches:
- NPC spawn
- navigation
- items
- factions
- combat
- crime
- shops
- timing

BAD:
Detailed restaurant-management simulator
